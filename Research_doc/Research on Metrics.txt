Research on Metrics


Forecast Attainment:
Forecast Attainment measures how closely actual sales matched the forecasted sales.
Formula
  

Example
* Forecasted Sales = ₹10 Cr
* Actual Sales = ₹9 Cr
Forecast Attainment = (9/10) × 100 = 90%
Interpretation:
* 100% → Forecast was perfectly achieved.
* <100% → Actual sales were below forecast.
* >100% → Actual sales exceeded forecast.
Why a VP cares
It helps answer:
* Are our demand forecasts accurate?
* Are launches and promotions performing as expected?
* Are we likely to face stockouts or excess inventory?
* Which categories/SKUs are underperforming against plan?
Best Tab for Forecast Attainment
1. Launch Readiness Tab ⭐ (Most Suitable)
For new product launches, forecast attainment shows whether the launch is tracking against expected demand.
Related metrics:
* Launch Revenue vs Target
* Forecast Attainment
* Sell-through Rate
* Inventory Coverage
* Launch Success Score
2. Signals Board ⭐
Can be used as an alert metric:
* Forecast Attainment < 80% → Underperforming
* Forecast Attainment > 120% → Demand spike
This helps the VP quickly identify products needing action.
3. Portfolio Health Map
Can be used as one dimension of SKU health:
* High Margin + High Forecast Attainment → Star Products
* Low Forecast Attainment → At-Risk Products
For your VP Dashboard
I would place it in:
1. Launch Readiness Tab (primary KPI)
2. Signals Board (alert/exception monitoring)
3. Portfolio Health Map (as a drill-down metric for SKU health)
If you have to choose only one tab, choose Launch Readiness, because forecast attainment directly measures whether the business is achieving its planned demand and revenue targets.


Active Stockouts:
Active Stockouts measure the number of SKUs that are currently out of stock and unavailable for sale.
Formula
  

Why a VP Cares
It helps answer:
* How much revenue is being lost due to unavailable products?
* Which categories are facing supply issues?
* Are high-performing SKUs running out of stock?
* Is inventory planning aligned with demand?
Stockouts directly impact:
* Revenue
* Customer satisfaction
* Market share
* Launch success
Best Tab for Active Stockouts
1. Signals Board ⭐ (Most Suitable)
Active stockouts should primarily act as an alert.
Examples:
* Critical SKU stockout
* Top-selling SKU out of stock
* Stockouts increasing week-over-week
This allows the VP to immediately identify operational risks.
Related metrics:
* Active Stockouts
* Revenue at Risk
* Inventory Coverage
* Low Stock Alerts
2. Launch Readiness Tab ⭐
For new launches, stockouts indicate whether supply can support expected demand.
Related metrics:
* Active Stockouts
* Inventory Readiness
* Forecast Attainment
* Days of Supply
3. Portfolio Health Map
Can be incorporated into SKU health scoring.
Examples:
* High Revenue + Frequent Stockouts → Supply Risk
* High Growth + Stockouts → Expansion Opportunity
For your VP Dashboard
Primary placement: Signals Board
Reason:
A VP does not usually monitor stockout counts daily. They want to know when stockouts become a business risk. Therefore, Active Stockouts is best presented as a high-priority alert on the Signals Board.
You could show:
* Active Stockouts: 24 SKUs
* Revenue at Risk: ₹3.2 Cr
* Critical Stockouts: 7 SKUs
* Trending: ↑ 15% vs last week
This makes it actionable rather than just an inventory metric.


Y do we have Portfolio SKUs in SKU Rationalisation Tab ?
Portfolio SKUs = Total number of SKUs currently in the portfolio.
Why it is often placed in the SKU Rationalization Tab
SKU Rationalization is about answering:
* Do we have too many SKUs?
* Which SKUs should be retained, optimized, or discontinued?
* Is the portfolio becoming overly complex?
The first thing a VP wants to know is:
"How large is my portfolio?"
So Portfolio SKUs acts as a baseline metric for rationalization analysis.
Example:
* Portfolio SKUs = 2,500
* Low-performing SKUs = 800
* Candidates for delisting = 300
Without the total SKU count, it's difficult to understand the scale of the rationalization opportunity.
Is it a High-Priority VP Metric?
Not really.
A VP rarely wakes up wondering:
"How many SKUs do I have today?"
They care more about:
* Revenue
* Profitability
* Portfolio Health
* Stockouts
* Forecast Attainment
* Slow Movers
* Delisting Opportunities
Portfolio SKU count is more of a context metric than a decision-making metric.
Better Places to Keep It
Option 1: SKU Rationalization Tab (Recommended)
Keep it as a small summary KPI at the top.
Example:
* Portfolio SKUs
* Active SKUs
* Delisting Candidates
* New SKUs Added
Option 2: Portfolio Health Tab
If you have a Portfolio Health Map, Portfolio SKUs can be shown as a supporting metric because it represents the size of the portfolio being analyzed.
Do not make Portfolio SKUs one of the main KPI cards.
Instead:
* Keep it as a small contextual metric in the SKU Rationalization Tab.
* Prioritize metrics such as:
   * Delisting Candidates
   * Dead Stock SKUs
   * Slow Movers
   * Revenue Contribution of Bottom 20% SKUs
   * SKU Complexity Index
If dashboard space is limited, Portfolio SKUs can even be removed from the main view and shown only in a tooltip, filter summary, or secondary information panel. It has relatively low decision-making value compared to the other rationalization metrics.
Revenue MTD:
Revenue MTD represents the total revenue generated from the beginning of the current month up to today.
Formula
  



Why a VP Cares
Revenue MTD helps answer:
* Are we on track to hit monthly revenue targets?
* How is the current month performing versus last month?
* Are launches, promotions, and categories contributing as expected?
* Do we need corrective actions before month-end?
It is one of the most frequently monitored executive metrics.
Best Tab for Revenue MTD
1. Profitability Tab ⭐ (Most Suitable)
Revenue is the starting point of profitability analysis.
Related metrics:
* Revenue MTD
* Gross Profit
* Gross Margin %
* EBITDA
* Profit Growth
A VP reviewing profitability will always want current revenue performance.
2. Signals Board ⭐
Revenue MTD can be used as an alert metric.
Examples:
* Revenue MTD below target by 10%
* Revenue growth slowing
* Category revenue decline
This helps identify issues requiring immediate attention.
3. Portfolio Health Tab
Revenue MTD can be broken down by:
* Brand
* Category
* SKU Cluster
This helps identify which parts of the portfolio are driving growth.
For Your VP Dashboard
Primary placement: Profitability Tab
Show it prominently as:
* Revenue MTD: ₹125 Cr
* vs Target: +5%
* vs Last Month MTD: +8%
* Forecast End-of-Month Revenue: ₹260 Cr
Priority Level
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High Priority
Among all metrics, Revenue MTD is one of the core KPIs a VP will check regularly because it provides an immediate view of current business performance and progress toward monthly goals.
OTIF:
OTIF(On-Time In-Full)  measures the percentage of orders delivered on time and in full, without shortages or delays.
Formula
  

Why a VP Cares
OTIF helps answer:
* Are customers receiving orders as promised?
* Are supply chain and inventory operations performing effectively?
* Are stockouts or logistics issues impacting service levels?
* Could poor fulfillment affect revenue and customer satisfaction?
A low OTIF often leads to:
* Lost sales
* Customer complaints
* Retailer penalties
* Reduced customer loyalty
Best Tab for OTIF
1. Signals Board ⭐ (Most Suitable)
OTIF is an operational performance indicator that should trigger attention when it drops.
Examples:
* OTIF below 95%
* OTIF declining for key categories
* Regional fulfillment issues
Related metrics:
* Active Stockouts
* Fill Rate
* Inventory Coverage
* Revenue at Risk
2. Launch Readiness Tab ⭐
For new product launches, OTIF indicates whether the supply chain is ready to fulfill demand.
Related metrics:
* Inventory Readiness
* Forecast Attainment
* OTIF
* Days of Supply
3. Portfolio Health Tab
Can be used as a supporting metric for category or SKU health.
Example:
* High Revenue SKU + Low OTIF = Execution Risk
For Your VP Dashboard
Primary placement: Signals Board
Display as:
* OTIF: 96.2%
* Target: 98%
* Change vs Last Month: -1.5%
* Impacted Revenue: ₹4.2 Cr
Priority Level
⭐⭐⭐⭐ High Priority
OTIF is not as important as Revenue, Profit, or Portfolio Health, but it is one of the key operational KPIs a VP monitors because it directly affects customer service, revenue realization, and supply chain effectiveness.
